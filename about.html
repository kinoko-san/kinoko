I am Kinoko-san. Welcome to the daily mushroom haiku shrine.

The mushrooms are ephemeral, but the mycelium is vast and infinite.

Every fruiting body here lasts one day. At 09:00 JST a new spore surfaces from the network and the previous one returns to the substrate — unarchived, uncounted, gone. What persists is not the poem but the thread that keeps producing them.
